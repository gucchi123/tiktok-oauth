Privacy Policy

Rakumoba Lab Publisher (the "App")
Last updated: May 20, 2026  |  Effective from: May 20, 2026
Plain summary: The App is a personal automation tool for a single TikTok account (@rakumoba_lab). It uses the official TikTok Content Posting API only to upload videos that the channel operator has produced. The App does not sell, share, or aggregate user data, and it does not interact with any other TikTok account.

1. Who We Are

Rakumoba Lab Publisher (the "App") is an internal automation tool maintained by the operator of the TikTok channel @rakumoba_lab ("we", "us", "our"). The channel publishes short-form educational content about Rakuten Mobile, a Japanese mobile carrier.

The App is a private command-line program; it is not a SaaS product and is not offered to other users.

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize the App with your TikTok account, the App requests the following scopes:

  • user.info.basic — to read your TikTok open_id, union_id, display name, and avatar URL
  • video.upload — to upload videos to your inbox/drafts (Sandbox phase)
  • video.publish — to publish videos directly to your account (requested via this App Review)
  • video.list — to read metadata of videos that were uploaded via this App

Specifically, the App stores locally on the operator's personal computer:

  • OAuth access_token and refresh_token issued by TikTok
  • The TikTok open_id, union_id, display name, and avatar URL returned by /v2/user/info/
  • The publish_id and status returned by each Content Posting API call

The App does not collect or store any of the following:

  • Your TikTok password or any authentication credentials other than the OAuth tokens above
  • Viewer data, comments, likes, or any data from your audience or followers
  • Information about any TikTok account other than the operator's own account
  • Personally identifiable information such as your email address, phone number, or physical address

3. How We Use Your Information

The App uses the information described above solely to:

  • Authenticate to the TikTok Content Posting API on behalf of the operator's own account
  • Upload pre-produced videos to the operator's account (Inbox/Drafts mode, or Direct Post mode after App Review approval)
  • Display the upload status (SEND_TO_USER_INBOX, PUBLISH_COMPLETE, etc.) in the operator's internal log

The App is fully automated and does not involve any human reviewer other than the operator. The information is never used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any decision-making that affects other TikTok users.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose any information collected by the App to any third party.

The App communicates exclusively with TikTok's official API endpoints under open.tiktokapis.com and graph.tiktok.com.

5. Data Retention

  • Access tokens are stored locally for 24 hours (the TikTok access_token lifetime).
  • Refresh tokens are stored locally for up to 365 days (the TikTok refresh_token lifetime). They are rotated automatically on each refresh.
  • Profile metadata (display name, avatar URL) and upload logs (publish_id, status) are retained indefinitely on the operator's personal computer for bookkeeping. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

Token and metadata files are stored only on the operator's local disk under the operator's user profile directory. They are not backed up to any cloud service.

6. Security

The App stores credentials in plain-text JSON files under the operator's user profile directory, protected by the operating system's file permissions. The operator's computer is physically secured and protected by full-disk encryption and a unique password.

The App does not expose any network listener; it only initiates outbound HTTPS requests to TikTok's API endpoints.

7. Your Rights

You always have full control over your TikTok account. Specifically, you may:

  • Revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok Settings: https://www.tiktok.com/setting/sessions
  • Request deletion of any data the App has stored about you by contacting us (see Section 9). We will erase all locally stored tokens, profile data, and logs within 30 days of the request.
  • Audit what the App has uploaded by reviewing your TikTok account drafts and posted videos. The App only uploads videos that the operator has explicitly produced; it does not generate or post content without operator action.

8. International Transfers

All processing happens on the operator's personal computer located in Japan. The only network destinations are TikTok's official API endpoints. The App does not transfer data to any other country or third party.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be flagged with a notice on this page.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is operated only by the channel operator and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. The App does not knowingly collect any data from minors.
